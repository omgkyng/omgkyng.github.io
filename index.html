GvMAGA Studio

GvMAGA Studio is a private personal tool that turns selected photos and screenshots into narrated videos. The batch workflow being completed supports up to 25 images, ordered clips and 1.35× playback with pitch preservation. Optional Gmail delivery sends the video, title, caption and summary to the owner-approved recipient. Unattended processing and email delivery are still being set up and verified. This informational website does not provide access to private uploads, jobs or videos.
