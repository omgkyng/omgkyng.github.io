Privacy policy

Updated October 21, 2026.

The Studio processes selected images, extracted or edited text, voice settings, generated media and accompanying titles, captions and summaries. Screenshots may contain personal information. Account identifiers control access; job records, hashes, checkpoints, usage records and diagnostic logs support processing and recovery. Browser storage retains preferences and pending intake identifiers.

OpenAI processes screenshot content and relevant text; optional fact checking may use web searches. ElevenLabs processes narration text and voice settings. Google Cloud and the Sites platform provide application hosting, processing and storage. Disabling API response storage does not guarantee immediate deletion of every provider record.

Gmail requests send-only permission, not inbox, contacts or calendar access. The app restricts delivery to the configured owner-approved recipient. Credentials are stored in Google Secret Manager, used only for authorized delivery, and not sent to extraction or narration providers. Send records help prevent duplicates. Email acceptance does not guarantee inbox delivery; sent emails and downloaded copies may remain with their recipients.

Cloud media becomes eligible for deletion after seven days; deletion is asynchronous and soft-deleted copies may remain another seven days. Control records and some other application records have no automatic expiry. Revoking Google access or clearing browser storage does not delete existing server records. Available removal controls and privacy requests can be used for retained data; provider-held records may require separate handling.

These informational pages add no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages records visitor IP addresses for security. Studio content stays private.
